Stan Alcorn…

Audio Writing Video Etc. Awards Contact

… has been making radio since 2010, when, in the span of a few months, he took a workshop at the Duke Center for Documentary Studies, attended the Third Coast International Audio Festival, co-founded the NYC Radio Club, and got a job at public radio's Marketplace.

Since then, he's reported local, national, and international news; launched, shaped, edited and sound designed new shows; and taught radio writing and story structure to countless print reporters. Somewhere in there he wrote a surprisingly influential article about why audio doesn't go viral, and gave a significantly less influential talk about why radio storytelling is, in fact, bad. His specialty remains turning complicated investigations into compelling narrative documentaries.
