Stan Alcorn

Audio Writing Video Etc. Awards Contact

Stan is an award-winning investigative reporter, editor and audio maker. Lately, he has been reporting from Colombia on the relationship between human beings and nature, with support from the Alicia Patterson Fellowship, the McGraw Fellowship for Business Journalism, and the Earth Journalism Network.

Previously, Stan was one of the founding reporter-producers at Reveal, the weekly, narrative, national public radio show of investigative reporting. In his seven years there, he shaped the show’s sound and systems and made stories that were taught in university classrooms and cited in Congress, inspired changes in law and won numerous awards.

Prior to Reveal, he was a staff reporter at Marketplace, WNYC and Fast Company; helped launch new shows at Slate, Marketplace and WNYC; shot, directed, and edited short documentary films; and helped create what is now GCJT.org, an online resource for journalists who cover violence.
